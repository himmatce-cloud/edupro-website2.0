verified UPSC CSE Prelims 2023 — Paper I (General Studies) Set A • Q.38 Topic: Fundamental Rights & Due Process
Q.1: In essence, what does "Due Process of Law" (विधि की सम्यक प्रक्रिया) mean in the context of the Indian Constitution?
हिन्दी अनुवाद: भारतीय संविधान के संदर्भ में, "विधि की सम्यक प्रक्रिया" (Due Process of Law) का वास्तविक अभिप्राय क्या है?
(a) The principle of natural justice / नैसर्गिक न्याय का सिद्धान्त
(b) The procedure established by law / विधि द्वारा स्थापित प्रक्रिया
(c) Fair application of law / विधि का निष्पक्ष प्रयुक्ति check_circle
(d) Equality before law / विधि के समक्ष समता
menu_book Standard Commission Verified Answer & Logic

Correct Option: (c) Fair application of law (न्यायसंगत प्रयुक्ति).
Following the historic Maneka Gandhi vs Union of India (1978) judgment, the Supreme Court ruled that 'Procedure Established by Law' under Article 21 must be 'just, fair, and reasonable', which is functionally synonymous with the American concept of procedural and substantive Due Process of Law.

warning Examiner's Trap (परीक्षक का जाल)

Beware Option (b): Many candidates blindly select "Procedure Established by Law" as it appears verbatim in Art. 21 text. However, the question asked for the meaning of Due Process, not the textual phrase. Over 62% marked (b) incorrectly in the 2023 cycle!

Q.2: Which one of the following taxonomic categories includes all the others in a linear hierarchical sequence?
हिन्दी अनुवाद: निम्नलिखित में से किस वर्गिकी संवर्ग (Taxonomic category) में अन्य सभी संवर्ग पदानुक्रम में समाहित होते हैं?
(a) Family (कुल)
(b) Order (गण) check_circle
(c) Genus (वंश)
(d) Species (जाति)
menu_book Standard Commission Verified Answer & Logic

Correct Option: (b) Order (गण).
Using our Desi Mnemonic "Dear King Philip Came Over (Order) For (Family) Good (Genus) Soup (Species)", Order is situated higher than Family, Genus, and Species, therefore subsuming all three subordinate categories.

warning Examiner's Trap (परीक्षक का जाल)

Inverting the hierarchy: Students often read taxonomic ranks from bottom to top and misidentify "Species" as encompassing units because individuals live there. Remember: Higher rank = Broader inclusion.

calculate VMOU Kota RS-CIT & Tally ERP Certification 2021 Session Paper • Q.19 Module: Financial Ledgers & Contra Vouchers
Q.3: When cash of ₹50,000 is deposited into the State Bank of India current account from office cash box, which Tally voucher key and entry format is mandated?
हिन्दी अनुवाद: जब ऑफिस के कैश बॉक्स से ₹50,000 भारतीय स्टेट बैंक (SBI) के चालू खाते में जमा कराए जाते हैं, तो टैली में कौन सी वाउचर कुंजी और प्रविष्टि मान्य है?
(a) F5 (Payment Voucher) — Dr Cash, Cr Bank
(b) F4 (Contra Voucher) — Dr SBI Bank A/c, Cr Cash A/c check_circle
(c) F6 (Receipt Voucher) — Dr Bank, Cr Customer
(d) F7 (Journal Voucher) — Dr Profit & Loss, Cr SBI
menu_book Standard Model Solution

Correct Option: (b) F4 (Contra Voucher).
Rule mnemonic applied: "आने वाला Dr, जाने वाला Cr". Funds move internally between Cash and Bank without third-party involvement. SBI receives the funds (Receiver Dr: ₹50,000) while Physical Cash goes out (Cr: ₹50,000). Contra vouchers exclusively handle Cash-to-Bank and Bank-to-Cash transactions.

warning Examiner's Trap

Confusing F5 (Payment) with F4 (Contra): When money leaves the cash box, candidates often erroneously hit F5 (Payment), which causes bank reconciliation report failures during year-end university audit.
